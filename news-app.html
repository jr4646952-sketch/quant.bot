    How it reads a headline

    BULL / BEAR comes from the words in the headline. Movement words flip with the subject: stocks rise is bullish, yields rise or inflation jumps is bearish. Fixed phrases like rate cut, tariffs, ceasefire or misses estimates always mean the same thing. Questions and headlines with no clear words are marked unsure.

    !!! = usually moves the Nasdaq a lot (Fed, CPI, jobs, tariffs, Nvidia, big tech). ! = usually minor. It's keyword matching, not understanding, so always open the story before acting.
